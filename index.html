Expertise

Research in one hand, the P&L in the other

The gap in most companies isn't awareness of AI — it's practical use. That's the gap I close, and not only for AI: for every data technology the business already owns. I apply research to businesses — bringing what the science actually knows into functions that have to use it Monday morning — and I develop the same people to get measurably better in both halves of the job: the technology and the leadership.

Patterns — from architecture to the anatomy of the firm

Technology has proven architecture patterns; so does everything around it — how teams are shaped, how change takes hold, how business model and process connect to data. I diagnose them together, as one anatomy. Structure creates outcomes: everyone says they want to be data-driven; few build the structure that produces it. That's why the fix is usually not another platform.

Designing the data you'll wish you had

A "data problem" is usually a problem about how the company sees itself — what counts as a customer, where the margin sits. I know what to ask the data, which answers to mistrust — and that missing data can't be backfilled. So I design collection and capture — process events, customer signals, IoT and sensor data — for two readers: your people, and the AI agents that help them run the business. Both need to know, fast, what's actually going on.

AI and decision systems for operations

Decision-intelligence layers on production, operations, and customer data — systems that help people see earlier and decide faster. Especially in manufacturing and industrial environments, where data and decisions are most directly tied.

Data & AI governance, non-invasive

Data treated as a product, ownership inside operations, governance that formalises the accountability already in the business. The discipline I also serve on the board of DAMA Czech Republic.

Teams that survive me — and experts I don't have to search for

Engineers, analysts, and platform people who deliver in operationally-led environments. When the work needs a true specialist, I bring in someone I've known for years — not a CV from a database. And I work through the incumbent CIO and IT leadership, not around them.
